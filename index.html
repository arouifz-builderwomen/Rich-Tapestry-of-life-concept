THREAD I

Every tapestry begins with a single thread.

We spend our lives searching. Sometimes for adventure. Sometimes for silence. Often without knowing which one we truly need.

Then, almost without noticing, something begins to change.

The mornings become familiar. The landscape remembers your footsteps. Conversations last a little longer. Time slows to its natural rhythm.

"Let yourself be silently drawn by the strange pull of what you really love."
— Jalal al-Din Rumi
